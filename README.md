Reserve-gated momentum bot for the pump.fun replay: it waits until a fresh coin's real SOL reserves first cross 12 SOL, then buys a small fixed slice (at most 0.90% of real reserves, capped at 0.10 SOL). It exits on take-profit (+30%), stop-loss (-15%), a trailing stop once 20% in profit, a 2000-slot max hold, or the bonding curve completing. One trade per coin, at most five open positions, every order sized under 1% of real reserves. It speaks the replay protocol directly over NDJSON on stdin/stdout — deterministic, no network, no randomness, no clock.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
